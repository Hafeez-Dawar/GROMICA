Supported Analyses

📈 RMSD

Root Mean Square Deviation for protein stability analysis. Supports both whole protein and protein-ligand complexes.

📊 RMSF

Root Mean Square Fluctuation to identify flexible regions. Includes residue highlighting feature.

🔵 Radius of Gyration

Measure protein compactness and structural changes over time.

💧 SASA

Solvent Accessible Surface Area to analyze protein-solvent interactions.

🔗 Hydrogen Bonds

Track hydrogen bond formation and stability throughout your simulation.
