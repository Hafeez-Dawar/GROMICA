Analysis Toolkit

Five Essential Tools, Infinite Possibilities

Comprehensive molecular dynamics analysis at your fingertips

📈

RMSD Analysis

Assess protein stability with Root Mean Square Deviation. Full support for protein and protein-ligand complexes with detailed statistics.

📊

RMSF Analysis

Identify flexible regions and loop dynamics through Root Mean Square Fluctuation with advanced residue highlighting.

🔵

Radius of Gyration

Track protein compactness and structural changes over time with comprehensive statistical analysis.

💧

SASA Calculation

Analyze Solvent Accessible Surface Area to understand protein-solvent interactions and conformational dynamics.

🔗

Hydrogen Bond Analysis

Monitor hydrogen bond formation, stability, and dynamics throughout your entire simulation trajectory.
