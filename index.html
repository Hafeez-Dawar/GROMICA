Complete Toolkit

Five Essential Analyses

All the tools you need for comprehensive MD trajectory analysis

📈

RMSD Analysis

Root Mean Square Deviation for protein stability assessment. Full support for both whole protein and protein-ligand complex simulations.

📊

RMSF Analysis

Root Mean Square Fluctuation to identify flexible regions and loop dynamics. Features residue range highlighting for detailed analysis.

🔵

Radius of Gyration

Measure protein compactness and track structural changes over simulation time with comprehensive statistical output.

💧

SASA Analysis

Solvent Accessible Surface Area calculation to analyze protein-solvent interactions and conformational changes.

🔗

Hydrogen Bonds

Track hydrogen bond formation, stability, and dynamics throughout your molecular dynamics simulation trajectory.
